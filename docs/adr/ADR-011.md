# ADR-011: Confidence Score — Single 0-100 Composite Replacing Renewal Risk

**Date:** 2026-04-04
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Jason Horn

---

## Context

The original design proposed separate signals: Health Score (existing 6-signal weighted score), Renewal Risk (a separate concept for subscription proximity), and potentially a Churn Risk indicator.

The concern: "simple first, no information overload." Multiple overlapping risk scores create cognitive load and require explanation. The SA needs a single number to scan down the customer list and know where to focus.

Renewal Risk as a standalone concept also had a scoping problem — it only reflected subscription proximity, ignoring interaction recency and case health, which are equally important indicators of an account going sideways.

---

## Decision

**Single Confidence Score (0-100) replacing separate Renewal Risk.**

`src/health-score.ts` exports `computeConfidenceScore()`. It returns a `ConfidenceScoreBreakdown` with four sub-components:

| Component | Max Points | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Sub proximity | 40 | Days until nearest active subscription expiry |
| Interaction recency | 30 | Days since last email or meeting interaction |
| Case health | 20 | Severity of open support cases |
| Pipeline presence | 10 | Whether active pipeline opportunities exist |

**Score interpretation:**
- 90-100: Strong account health, low near-term risk
- 70-89: Generally healthy, minor concerns
- 50-69: Watch list — one or more signals degraded
- Below 50: Action required

**Sub proximity scoring (0-40):**
- Expired: 0
- ≤30d: 5
- ≤60d: 15
- ≤90d: 25
- >90d: 40

**Interaction recency scoring (0-30):**
- >90d silent: 0
- 61-90d: 8
- 31-60d: 18
- 15-30d: 24
- ≤14d: 30

**Case health scoring (0-20):**
- Sev1 open: 0
- Sev2 open: 8
- Sev3/4 only: 14
- No open cases: 20

**Pipeline presence (0-10):**
- Has active pipeline: 10
- No pipeline: 5

Free/trial subscriptions are excluded from sub proximity calculation via `isFreeOrTrial()`.

---

## Consequences

- SA sees one number per customer on the `AccountPortfolioGrid` — no cognitive overload
- Tooltip breakdown available in UI to explain the score components
- The existing 6-signal Health Score (Cases 25%, Subs 20%, Meetings 15%, Emails 15%, Pipeline 15%, Cloud 10%) is preserved — it remains the primary health indicator
- Confidence Score is additive — it surfaces alongside Health Score, not replacing it
- `computeConfidenceScore` is exported from `health-score.ts` and called from the customer API layer to attach `confidenceScore` to each customer object in the response
- Future: score weights can be tuned by adjusting the max points per component without changing API shape
