What You Will See

The platform collects signals from 9 data sources (support cases, emails, meetings, Drive docs, pipeline, subscriptions, cloud spend, RSS, events) and weaves them into intelligence outputs for each customer account. The demo account has rich signal coverage across cases, emails, subscriptions, and cross-reference connections — making it the ideal baseline to explore.

Step-by-Step: Explore, Refresh, and Compare

1

Open the Dashboard

Navigate to demo.jasonhorn.io/dashboard in your browser. You'll see the portfolio overview page showing all demo accounts as cards in a grid.

Running locally? Use localhost:7779 instead.

2

View the Baseline Brief

Click on the demo account card in the dashboard grid. This opens the customer detail page. Scroll through to read the current Customer Brief. This is the "before" state — what the platform generates from the existing baseline signals.

Take note of:

  • Which support cases are highlighted
  • What themes appear in the brief narrative
  • How many cross-reference connections are shown in the intelligence graph
3

Refresh Data Sources

Use the dashboard's Data Freshness panel to refresh any data source. Click Refresh on Cases, Pipeline, Subscriptions, or any other source. The platform re-pulls live data — new cases, updated pipeline opportunities, and changed subscriptions flow in automatically.

No file editing required. Everything happens through the UI.

4

Regenerate an Output

After refreshing data sources, click Regenerate on any consumer output:

  • Customer Brief — click the refresh icon on the Brief section
  • Account Plan — click Generate on the Account Plan tab
  • Meeting Prep — click an upcoming meeting, then Generate Prep
  • Product Intel — click Generate on any product card

The pipeline re-runs with the updated data. Watch the output change based on the new signals.

5

Compare Before and After

Navigate back to the customer account page and compare the updated outputs with what you saw in Step 2:

  • New data highlighted — refreshed cases, pipeline changes, or new email context should appear in the brief
  • Cross-references — the intelligence graph shows new connections between refreshed signals and existing data
  • Brief narrative — the text should now reflect the updated data and its business impact
  • Ripple effect — check other consumers (Account Plan, Meeting Prep, Campaigns) to see how the same data change affected multiple outputs
6

Explore Further

Now that you've seen a signal flow through the pipeline, try exploring other parts of the dashboard:

  • Intelligence Graph — click the graph visualization to see how signals connect to each other
  • Other Accounts — click on other account cards to see how different signal patterns produce different briefs
  • Product View — toggle to the product view to see signals grouped by Red Hat product line

What to Look For

When evaluating the platform, consider:

  • ▸ Signal Coverage — Does the brief capture all relevant signals for the account?
  • ▸ Cross-References — Are connections between signals meaningful and actionable?
  • ▸ Brief Quality — Is the narrative useful for preparing for a customer meeting?
  • ▸ Missing Signals — What information sources would make the brief more complete?
  • ▸ Confusing Labels — Is anything unclear, jargon-heavy, or hard to understand?
  • ▸ Hidden Context — What would you want to know about HOW the output was generated?

Providing Feedback

After exploring the platform, share your observations with the team. The most valuable feedback is:

What to submit (2–3 ideas via GitHub form by Wed Aug 27)

1. What was surprising or unexpected in the brief
2. What information was missing that you'd need for a real customer conversation
3. Which cross-references were most useful and which felt like noise
4. Ideas for new features, labels, layouts, or signal sources
5. Anything that confused you — every confusion is a potential improvement

Open an issue on the project GitHub repository, post in the Slack channel, or bring your feedback to the next team sync.
