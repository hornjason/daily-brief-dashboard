INNOVATION How creative and original?

FEASIBILITY Can it scale?

  • Already deployed and in daily use — not a prototype
  • Team submission → shipped workflow (hours, not sprints)
  • Container deployment, SSO via Cloudflare Access

USER EXPERIENCE Intuitive and useful?

  • One brief replaces 8 systems, under 2 minutes

TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE Thoughtful use of AI?

  • 17 quality validators — deterministic, not AI vibes
  • Auto-retry on quality failure (75% threshold)

  • 9 sources → 22 modules → cross-reference → 11 consumers
