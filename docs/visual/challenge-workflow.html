The Challenge Workflow

Add a document to Google Drive → regenerate → see every output change. This is how the team demonstrates innovation.

1

Capture Baseline

Open a customer account in the dashboard. Read the current brief and account plan. This is your "before" state — note the priority action, signal count, graph edges, and quality score.

Dashboard → Customer Account → Account Plan
Quality Score: 90% | Sections: 12 | Initiatives: 3
Priority Action: "Review Strategic POV document"
2

Add a Document to Google Drive

This is the primary way to change inputs. Drop any document into the customer's shared Drive folder — the pipeline reads it on the next regeneration and synthesizes it into every consumer output automatically.

Competitive Alert
Drop a competitive intelligence brief into Drive — a vendor switch, a migration trend, a market shift
Effect: New initiative appears in account plan, competitive context updates across all consumers
Meeting Notes
Drop notes from a customer conversation — decisions made, concerns raised, next steps discussed
Effect: Meeting context flows into brief, talking points update, engagement timeline enriches
Partner Update
Drop a partner relationship update — joint solutions, co-sell opportunities, integration news
Effect: Partner context appears in account plan, new opportunities surface in expansion motions
Market Analysis
Drop an industry analysis, analyst report summary, or market trend document
Effect: Industry context enriches, competitive positioning shifts, strategic framing adapts

Anyone on the team can do this. No code, no config — just drag a file into Google Drive.

Verified: adding a migration alert produced a new consolidation initiative. Removing it and regenerating removed the initiative. The system reads fresh every time.

3

Regenerate

Click regenerate on any consumer output. The pipeline re-runs with the new Drive document included. Gemini reads the document, extracts intelligence, and weaves it into the output.

After adding a competitive alert to Drive:
signal-loader: loaded (2,800 tokens)
intelligence: loaded (5,000 tokens)
drive-docs: loaded (1,200 tokens — NEW from your document)
solution-plays: loaded (1,200 tokens)
Quality gate: 94/75 PASS
