Walk into every customer conversation prepared — with AI-powered briefs built from 9 data sources, cross-referenced in real time.
What Is This?

An AI-powered intelligence platform that produces customer intelligence briefs for Red Hat Account Solution Architects. It aggregates signals from Salesforce, support cases, Gmail, Calendar, Drive, cloud marketplace, Red Hat RSS, events, and product lifecycle — then cross-references them with weighted confidence scoring to surface what matters and what to do about it.

This is real and running, not a prototype. The demo environment includes a full account with real cases, emails, subscriptions, and hundreds of graph edges.

How the Brief Gets Built
1
Bootstrap
Creates Drive folder structure, imports Salesforce bookings, discovers customers from shared data source
2
Intelligence Generation
Gemini + Google Search grounding produces company intelligence docs and industry analysis on Day 0
3
9 Data Sources
Cases, Emails, Meetings, Drive Docs, Pipeline, Subscriptions, Cloud Spend, Red Hat RSS, Events — each with its own scraper, refresh timer, and cache
4
22 Processing Modules
7 AI-powered (Gemini) + 15 data transformation modules that enrich, cross-reference, and route signals
5
Cross-Reference Engine
Weighted confidence scoring with specificity tiers, 8 score boosters, budget caps, and 30-day time decay
6
Quality Gate
17 programmatic validators · 75% threshold — deterministic structural checks, not AI. Below threshold = auto-regenerate
✓
11 Consumers
Customer Brief, Account Plan, Meeting Prep, Campaigns, Playbook, Value Positioning, Expansion Opps, and 4 more — same data, different slices

Explore the Demo

1. View the Baseline

Open the dashboard → click the demo account card → read the brief. Note signal counts, graph edges, and quality score.

2. Change an Input

Use the dashboard's Data Freshness panel to refresh any data source (Cases, Pipeline, Subscriptions). Or click Generate on Intelligence, Account Plan, or Meeting Prep to trigger a fresh generation.

3. Regenerate

Click the refresh icon on the Customer Brief or hit Generate on the Account Plan. The pipeline re-runs with updated inputs.

4. Compare Before / After

Return to the customer page → observe changes in the brief, updated graph edges, new recommended actions reflecting the refreshed data.

5. Check the Ripple Effect

The same data change updates multiple consumers. Check: did the Brief's recommended actions change? Did the Account Plan score shift? Did Meeting Prep talking points update?
