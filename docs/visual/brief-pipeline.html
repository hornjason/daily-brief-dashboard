How the Brief Gets Built

Brief Encounters — the intelligence pipeline from raw signals to actionable briefings

Stage 1
Bootstrap

Account Setup

When an AE is added, the system creates Drive folders, pulls the customer list from the territory sheet, and connects Salesforce. This is the foundation — everything else builds on it.

Stage 2
Intelligence

AI Research (Gemini + Google Search)

For every new customer, Gemini generates a Company Intelligence doc and an Industry Analysis doc using Google Search grounding. These aren't templates — they're real research, created immediately at Day 0.

Stage 3
Signals

7 Signal Sources

Signals accumulate over time. Each source has its own scraper, refresh timer, and cache. More time = richer brief.

Stage 4
Cross-Ref

Cross-Reference Engine

Weighted confidence scoring connects signals across sources. A security advisory matches to a customer running the affected version, cross-referenced with their renewal in 47 days.

Stage 5
Quality

Quality Gate (11 Checks)

Every generated output runs through an 11-check quality validator. Below threshold = regenerate. Gemini multimodal reads the scoring rubric as a PDF image for structural validation.
