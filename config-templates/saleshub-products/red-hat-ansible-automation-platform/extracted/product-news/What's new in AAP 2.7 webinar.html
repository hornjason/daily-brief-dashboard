What’s new with Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform 2.7

Share

  • Jump to section
Jump to section

Ansible Automation Platform 2.7 brings a fleet of new capabilities designed to improve IT efficiency and reduce friction across every stage of your automation journey–from first automated task to enterprise-wide scale and more autonomous operations. Join our expert-led webinar to discover the latest enhancements designed to optimize automation service delivery, accelerate adoption, and implement AI-driven IT operations while ensuring governance and control. 

You'll learn:

  • How the new self-service automation portal and visual execution environment builder simplify content development and enforce consistency.
  • What modernized identity management with OIDC and short-lived tokens means for automation in zero trust environments.
  • How "bring-your-own-knowledge" capabilities within the automation intelligent assistant help you adhere to your organization's standards and practices.
  • How the MCP server for Ansible Automation Platform enables an agentic, natural-language approach to fact gathering, troubleshooting, and job execution.  

 

Live event date: Tuesday, July 14, 2026 | 10:00 a.m. ET

On-demand event: Available for one year afterward
