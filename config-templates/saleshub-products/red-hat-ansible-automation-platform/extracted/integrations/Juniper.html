Bring together knowledge and expertise with Red Hat and Juniper

Connect with ideas and compete across organizations with Juniper Networks Junos OS and Red Hat® Ansible® Automation Platform.

Overview

Together, Ansible Automation Platform and Juniper’s automation and orchestration tools provide the knowledge and expertise of experienced IT solutions developers, operators, and administrators.

The joint solution addresses the most common applications of automation and orchestration in the management and provisioning of IT resources, including configuration automation, test-driven networking, and continuous compliance.

It includes Ansible modules developed specifically for Juniper Networks Junos OS, that can connect to the network via NETCONF or Command Line Interface via secure socket shell (SSH). This specific collection can be used for different Juniper devices, as Junos OS uses the same syntax across all Juniper hardware including firewalls, routing, and switching (for datacenter or access).
