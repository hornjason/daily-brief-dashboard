# CY27 Account Planning Playbook — Continuous Account Planning Insights (CAPI)

## What Is Changing for CY27

Red Hat is evolving to Continuous Account Planning Insights (CAPI) — shifting from a once-a-year exercise to ongoing visibility into account planning progress and quality. Plans are now anchored to the CUSTOMER's fiscal cycle, not Red Hat's, with quarterly review cadence and monthly visibility reporting. This starts July 2026. 70% of customers have a January fiscal year, so most planning work occurs around August/September.

Key changes:
- Annual reporting moves to continuous reporting with monthly visibility and progress reports
- Plans extend a full year ahead on a rolling basis, reviewed quarterly
- Create new plan 5 months prior to customer fiscal year start (clone previous year plan)
- Automated email notifications trigger AE to start the exercise
- Self-service dashboards coming for real-time tracking

What stays the same: 1x per year you clone the previous year plan. 2027 is an update, not a rebuild.

## CY27 Minimum Documentation Requirements (13 Requirements)

An account plan is complete when ALL of these conditions are met:

### 1. Scorecard Freshness
- 60%+ of scorecard answers updated in past quarter
- Limited one-word or low-value responses
- No missing answers
- Use GEMs to gather meaningful data and insights

### 2. Core Requirements
1. New upcoming fiscal year plan marked as primary
2. At least TWO customer initiatives in the Initiative section
3. At least ONE potential tied to any initiative
4. If IBM Co-Sell account: one initiative MUST be an IBM initiative (type "IBM" in initiative name field; if no co-sell, add "Nothing IBM")
5. Scorecard score over 85%
6. At least 1 Customer Business Unit in Whitespace Map
7. At least 1 account map marked as primary with at least 2 customer contacts
8. At least 1 contact marked as Champion in primary Account Map
9. NEW CY27: At least 1 contact marked as Economic Buyer in primary Account Map (should be different person from Champion in most cases)
10. At least 1 contact marked for NPS survey

### 3. NEW CY27 Scorecard Requirements
11. Ecosystem section Q#4 (NEW): "How do you plan to leverage Services Partners, Value-Added Resellers, and Distributors to drive growth in this account?"
12. Technical Landscape Q#16 (UPDATED): Security, Compliance, Sovereignty & Accessibility — includes connection to Sovereignty and Lightwell motions
13. Economic Buyer identification in Account Map with proper role and support status markings

### Economic Buyer Identification (NEW CY27)

The Economic Buyer has:
- Power of Veto: Can say "yes" when others say no, and "no" when others are aligned
- P&L Ownership: Directly accountable for financial performance; has final authority over strategic investments
- Access to Discretionary Funds: Can reallocate or unlock funds for unplanned high-priority initiatives
- Strategic Focus: Evaluates solutions through business lens (cost, time-to-value, risk/bottom-line impact)

Champion and Economic Buyer should typically be DIFFERENT people. If same person, mark Champion as Support Status and Economic Buyer as Role.

## Customer Event Triggers — When to Update Your Plan

A customer change = a great time for a conversation and updating your plan.

| Trigger Category | Look For | Why It Matters | Your Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growth & Expansion | New products/services, new markets, hiring spikes | Growth creates gaps in systems, processes, scalability | Start conversation around supporting growth and scale |
| Financial Events | Earnings results, funding rounds, cost-cutting | Signals shifts in budget and executive focus | Align solutions to growth investment or cost optimization |
| Leadership/Org Changes | New CIO/CTO, restructuring | New leaders reset strategy and evaluate vendors | Reintroduce your value and explore new priorities |
| Strategic Announcements | Partnerships, innovation, AI, transformation | Indicates long-term investments and roadmap direction | Connect your solution to their stated strategy |
| Risk or Disruption | Security breaches, outages, negative press, layoffs | Pain creates urgency and faster decision-making | Position solutions around risk reduction and stability |
| Technology Signals | New tools/platforms, vendor changes, tech job postings | Reveals current stack, gaps, future direction | Offer guidance on integration, optimization, replacement |
| Planning & Budget Cycles | Fiscal year start/end, budget planning, renewals, QBRs | Timing impacts buying decisions as much as need | Engage early to influence planning and budget allocation |
| Industry Events | Regulatory changes, competitor moves, market shifts | External pressure drives internal change | Lead with insights on navigating industry change |

## Continuous Planning Cycle

### Standard Cadence
- Ongoing: Update as new activities, opportunities, or insights arise
- Quarterly (minimum): Review and refresh at least every 90 days
- Monthly: Progress monitored and reported for trends

### Planning Cycle
- ~5 months before customer FY start: AE receives automated email. Copy prior year plan, update with new requirements, mark as primary, review with manager
- 2-3 months before FY: Finalize and present plan to customer/partners

### Manager Approval Process (NEW CY27)
- Managers receive automated notifications when plans are ready for review
- Approval based on customer's fiscal year, not RH's
- Status workflow: Pending Review -> Approved / Rejected
- Rejection includes feedback; AE receives notification with corrections needed
- Status stays rejected until manager reviews re-submission

## Account Planning Phases

Account planning is segmented into 4 phases:

### Know Your Customer
- Customer Intelligence Gathering
- Technical Account Intelligence Gathering
- Customer Contacts Mapping (including Economic Buyer identification)
- Value of Partners Mapping
- Open Topics & Questions Gathering
- Lessons Learned & Retrospective Review
- Validation of Findings with Customer or Partners

### Know Your Fit
- Adoption Maturity & Customer Success Assessment
- Mapping our fit to customer initiatives (use 2x2 prioritization matrix)
- Connection to Sales Plays and TDPs
- Tagging Opportunities & Potentials
- New Business & Whitespace Ideas Brainstorming

### Build Your Strategy
- Account Ambitions & Goal Setting (1-year and 3-year)
- Identification of Risks
- Formulating Account Plan & Milestones
- Formulating Technical Plan
- Formulating Customer Success Plan
- Executive Sponsor engagement
- Partner Review, Feedback & Alignment
- Identifying need for Buying Programs (HCS, CSA, ESP)

### Make It Happen
- Assigning Actions & Orchestrate Execution
- Measuring Progress
- Reviewing Progress with Customer
- Updating the Plan continuously
- Coaching the Account Team
- Executive Support
- Red Carpet for Account Reassignments

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Core Account Team
- Account Executive (AE): Core team leader; owns account plan & execution. Leads all planning phases; drives execution & aligns resources. Builds team capability & sets ambitious goals. Ensures continuous, value-driven planning. Leads IBM co-sell initiatives when applicable.
- Account Solution Architect (ASA): Core team lead for technical & adoption strategy. Leads technical discovery, adoption maturity assessment. Builds technical & customer success plans. Defines roadmap to achieve business outcomes. Drives continuous improvement in planning quality.
- Customer Success Executive (CSE): Collaborates across all planning phases. Co-leads Adoption Maturity & Customer Success Assessments with ASA. Co-leads development of Customer Success Plan. Tracks initiative progress using telemetry and customer signals.

### Sales Manager
- Coaches AEs across all planning phases & execution. Drives ambitious goals, strategy, risk identification. Conducts quarterly/QBR account plan reviews. Drives continuous improvement & holds teams accountable. Enforces planning discipline & skill development.

### Extended Team
- Territory Services Manager, ABM Marketer, Partner Account Manager, Deal Specialists, Other RH Specialists, Executive Sponsor

## Scorecard Structure

The scorecard is the intelligence & strategy repository with 6 categories + approvals:

### 1. Account Plan Overview
- ACV+ Ambition (3-years) — numerical answer only
- ACV+ Goal (current FY) — numerical answer only
- Growth % (3-years) — numerical answer only
- Growth % (current FY) — numerical answer only
- Why Red Hat? & Account Growth Plan
- Account Plan Risks & Obstacles
- Overall maturity with Red Hat (5-point scale)
- Consumption status

### 2. Account Intelligence
- Executive Summary of customer intelligence (concise, for briefings)
- Customer Business Objectives, Challenges & Key Initiatives (detailed)
- Customer's Market & Competition (AccountGPT enabled)
- Innovation adopter persona (Rogers Diffusion: Innovator/Early Adopter/Early Majority/Late Majority/Laggard)

### 3. Customer's Ecosystem
- Executive summary of ecosystem strategy (key partners, influence, RH engagement, PPA/Commercial Focus Partners)
- NEW Q#4: How do you leverage Services Partners, VARs, and Distributors to drive growth?
- Cloud providers in use (multi-select)
- Committed spend agreements (multi-select)
- Cloud adoption level (single-select)
- Specialized partner types with context on RH collaboration

### 4. Key Stakeholders Engagement
- Executive summary of marketing & relationship-building activities (must cover RH Summit, EBC, network growth strategy)
- Relationship maturity assessment
- ABM plan reference (link in Files section)

### 5. Technical Landscape
- Structured by TDP/Sales Play alignment (2 questions per TDP: analysis + adoption maturity 0-5)
- UPDATED Q#16: Security, Compliance, Sovereignty & Accessibility (connection to Sovereignty and Lightwell motions)
- Key customer applications and workloads
- Hardware & Storage

### 6. Customer Success
- Executive summary of expected outcomes, risks, adoption resources
- RH engagement in adoption journey (multi-choice, for reporting)
- Success plan approval date with customer

### 7. Approvals (CY27 Process)
- Starting July 2026, managers receive automated notifications
- Approval tied to customer fiscal year cycle
- Status: Pending Review -> Approved/Rejected with feedback

## Initiatives — Customer-Centric View

Initiatives represent customer initiatives in CUSTOMER'S terms, not RH products.

Requirements:
- At least 2 customer initiatives with success metrics and measures
- At least 1 potential opportunity tagged to an initiative
- IBM Co-Sell accounts: at least 1 IBM joint initiative

Each initiative must have:
- Timeline and target value (attainable share of wallet)
- Success metrics with base and target measures
- Status: Blocked / At Risk / On Track
- Progress: Not Started / In Progress / Complete
- Business Owner (customer contact in RHSC)
- Key partners (NEW: can add partners to each initiative)
- Tagged potential and existing opportunities

Use the prioritization matrix (2x2: RH Value vs Customer Impact) to identify focus areas.

## Whitespace Map — RH-Centric View

Maps Red Hat TDPs against customer Business Units:
- Rows: RH TDPs + "Other" + "Transformational Services"
- Columns: Customer Business Units (organized by Groups if needed)
- Status per cell: Deployed / Competitive / Open / Competitor (blocked)
- Each cell tracks: aligned opportunities/potentials, competitors (share of wallet), license expiration

Requirements: At least 1 Customer Business Unit defined.

## Account Map Requirements (CY27)

- At least 1 map marked as Primary (required for reporting and exports)
- At least 2 customer contacts in organizational chart
- 1 contact marked as Champion (Role: Champion, Decision Influence: High/Optimal)
- 1 contact marked as Economic Buyer (NEW CY27) — see Economic Buyer identification criteria above
- 1 contact marked for NPS survey (Survey Preference checkbox)
- Can have multiple maps (per Business Unit, per Initiative)

## Manager Quarterly Measured Goals (NEW CY27 — Starting Q3)

Sales Managers must meet regularly with AEs to review account plans across:
1. Plan Quality & Completeness: Plans must be meaningful, complete, aligned to strategy — not checkbox exercises
2. Pipeline-to-Ambition Alignment: Validate pipeline creation/progression against stated ambition and quota
3. Review, Coach & Improve Execution: Quarterly reviews to coach AEs, enforce team-based planning, improve quality

## Collaboration & Continuous Improvement

Signals for assessing team collaboration:
- Scorecard Dashboard: Who is contributing to the plan
- Account Team Section: Who listed and when they last accessed the plan
- Whitespace Map Activity: Who is actively updating opportunity areas

Best practices:
- Prioritize cross-functional alignment (tech sales, services, marketing)
- Engage Extended Account Team in planning, not just execution
- Compare customer maturity (TDPs) with sales plays, pipeline, and opportunity strategy
- Use preset exports to review and share plans — avoid disconnected PowerPoints
- Keep source of truth in RHSC

## AI Tools for Account Planning

### Gemini GEMs (PlanIQ)
6 custom GEMs providing detailed analysis per scorecard section:
- PlanIQ: GEM for Account Intelligence
- PlanIQ: GEM for Ecosystem Strategy
- PlanIQ: GEM for Key Stakeholder Engagement & Account Map
- PlanIQ: GEM for Technical Landscape & Whitespace Map
- PlanIQ: GEM for Customer Success & Initiatives
- PlanIQ: GEM for Customer View & Account Growth

Usage: Upload account plan PDF, set model to Thinking, run. Upload additional documents for deeper analysis.

### SalesAI (people.ai)
Pre-set prompts for account updates:
- "I'm new to this account, catch me up"
- "What potential risks exist for this account?"
- Custom prompts about goal progress, champion identification, partner engagement

Limitations: Uses only people.ai email/calendar data + RHSC data. Does not access Whitespace Map or Account Map.

### NotebookLM for Continuous Improvement
1. Load meeting minutes + call transcripts + latest account plan PDF, prompt for update suggestions
2. Create briefing & onboarding documents for new team members, partners, executives
3. Mindmapping to uncover new connections and insights

### Shadowbot (Coming Soon)
AI prompts for Sales Managers (readiness, coaching) and AEs (readiness, audit, initiatives audit, tech landscape audit).

## IBM Co-Sell Account Planning

Co-sell accounts require:
- Tight collaboration with IBM counterparts across all 3 phases
- At least 1 IBM joint solution captured as Initiative (use exact naming convention with "IBM" prefix)
- Joint solutions aligned to: Virtualization, Automation, Multi Cloud/Modernization, AI
- Partner contacts mapped in Account Map
- Co-sell engagement documented in Ecosystem section

## Buying Programs Integration

### Committed Spend Programs (HCS, CSA)
- Check if account growth/volume correspond to program requirements
- Review Whitespace Map for multi-BU, multi-route, multi-product consumption
- Initiatives must capture clear Success Plan with customer success metrics

### Enterprise Subscription Program (ESP)
- Check for large single-product consumption with growth expected
- Evaluate competitive takeout or new product seeding opportunities
- Cost predictability and procurement simplification assessment

Engage Deal Management team early in account planning.

## Account Plan Updates Cadence

Update at least every 90 days. Key triggers:
- Customer or partner feedback
- Shifts in customer strategy, priorities, or business model
- Leadership or key stakeholder changes
- Market or industry changes
- Partner ecosystem updates
- Key milestones or lessons learned
- Insights from executive engagements

Key moments to review:
- Ahead of customer planning and budgeting cycles
- Before major customer events (QBRs, executive engagements)
- After completing key milestones or deliverables

## Executive Summaries & Exports

Export presets available in RHSC:
- Executive Summary: Questions summarizing each scorecard section, goals, initiatives — for briefing executives
- Customer View: Subset for customer verification
- High Level Overview: Multiple choice questions for quick perspective
