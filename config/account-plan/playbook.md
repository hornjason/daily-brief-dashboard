Progress & Update session - focused on reviewing progress in account plan execution and incorporating new information gathered from the customer and partners into the plan d. Retrospective session - focused on reviewing the lessons learned from the execution of the account strategy and revisiting the objectives, activities and account intelligence e. IBM Joint Account Planning Sessions - any of the above types of sessions, but focused on the particular areas of joint solutions offered with IBM f. Global team alignment sessions - specific sessions focused on alignment and level-setting for the global teams working on large international accounts; the objective typically will be sharing information HOME That is a lot of abbreviations right there ⤴ - if you are not familiar with some of them, check the Red Hat Abbreviations Dictionary Identify & Engage Participants Assign pre-work & set agenda Run the account planning session Activate the account plan execution Core Account Team is always participating Add Extended Account Team as needed & relevant for the particular customer Account planning sessions can have different focus, but always require pre-work from the team. The prioritization model can be represented by a 2x2 matrix with the strategic value of Red Hat in x-axis and the impact of the initiative on customer's business in the y-axis. Plotting the initiatives into the matrix and using that for prioritization: i. Initiatives in the bottom left quadrant are not relevant for the customer and RH does not add value → deprioritize ii. Initiatives in the top left quadrant are important for the customers, but RH does not have much potential to contribute → we should be aware of these initiatives (as they will probably tie in customer's budget, resources and focus), but should not focus on them since our value will not be sufficient (see if this is opportunity to have the partners leading?) iii. The top right quadrant is the sweet spot where RH value is high and the value of the initiative for the customer is also high → any initiative in this space is probably containing many potential opportunities iv. The bottom right quadrant is tricky: RH can add great value to initiatives in this space, but their value for the customer is not high, which can cause that they might be deprioritized; work in this space only in case that these initiatives have budget allocated for the current year and a clear, strong sponsor 5.
